## 60-Second Demo Script

**[0–5s]** Open the browser. Black screen. Boot log types itself out in JetBrains Mono:
`> INIT ECU... > LOADING FRAME.GLB... > CALIBRATING IMU...`
The RPM gauge sweeps from zero to 13,000. The redline zone ignites in red.

**[5–10s]** Model is ready. Hard wipe reveal — the RSV4 Factory materialises on screen.
The HUD locks into place: corner brackets, telemetry panel, scroll-progress bar.

**[10–20s]** Start scrolling. RPM climbs in real time as scroll velocity increases.
Speed readout ticks up. Gear indicator steps through 1 → 6.
The Part Navigator on the right tracks every section.

**[20–40s]** Scroll through ENGINE → FRAME → FRONT FORK → BRAKES → ELECTRONICS.
Each section: a spec panel slides in with verified numbers from Aprilia's own site.
Hover the 3D bike — the cursor ring expands and names the part.

**[40–55s]** Click `[06] WINGLETS` in the navigator. Lenis smooth-scrolls there instantly.
Point out the pitch: *"Downforce built in. Drag is an afterthought."*
Specs appear. The bike rotates to highlight the aero surfaces.

**[55–60s]** Scroll to COCKPIT — the final section. Show the mute button.
Hit it → engine sound cuts. Hit again → back on.
